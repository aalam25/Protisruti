# Protisruti — Project Features

## 1. Project Overview

**Protisruti** is an AI-powered educational and empowerment platform designed
to help women and children access personalized learning support, develop
useful skills, discover educational opportunities, and make progress toward
their educational and career goals.

The platform will use artificial intelligence to provide personalized,
accessible, and supportive educational assistance.

The project is being developed as a fellowship project with a focus on
using technology to address educational barriers and create meaningful
social impact.

---

## 2. Project Vision

The vision of Protisruti is to make educational guidance and learning
support more accessible to women and children, particularly for individuals
who may have limited access to personalized educational resources,
technology, mentoring, or career guidance.

Protisruti will act as an intelligent learning companion that can help users
understand subjects, develop skills, create learning plans, discover
opportunities, and track their progress.

The long-term vision is to develop Protisruti into a platform that combines
artificial intelligence, personalized learning, educational resources, and
opportunity discovery to support lifelong learning and empowerment.

---

## 3. Problem Statement

Many women and children face barriers to education and personal development.

These barriers may include:

- Limited access to personalized educational support
- Lack of affordable tutoring or mentoring
- Limited access to technology and digital learning resources
- Difficulty finding appropriate educational materials
- Lack of information about scholarships and learning opportunities
- Limited career and educational guidance
- Language barriers
- Difficulty creating and following personalized learning plans
- Lack of tools for tracking learning progress

Traditional educational resources may not always provide individualized
support based on a learner's specific needs, interests, and goals.

Protisruti aims to address some of these challenges by providing an
accessible digital platform that can personalize learning assistance and
connect users with useful educational resources.

---

# 4. Target Users

Protisruti will initially focus on two primary groups:

## 4.1 Children and Students

Protisruti will support students who need additional learning assistance
outside of traditional classroom environments.

Potential users may include students who:

- Struggle with specific academic subjects
- Need additional practice
- Want personalized explanations
- Need help creating study plans
- Want to improve their reading or vocabulary
- Want to prepare for academic assessments
- Need help identifying areas where they need additional practice

### Potential Benefits for Students

Students will be able to:

- Ask educational questions
- Receive personalized explanations
- Generate practice questions
- Create study plans
- Practice academic concepts
- Improve reading and vocabulary
- Track learning progress
- Set educational goals

---

## 4.2 Women

Protisruti will support women who want to continue their education,
develop new skills, explore careers, or find educational opportunities.

Potential users may include women who:

- Want to develop digital skills
- Want to learn computer programming
- Want to improve English or communication skills
- Want to continue their education
- Need career guidance
- Want to discover scholarships
- Want to find free educational resources
- Want to develop professional skills

### Potential Benefits for Women

Women will be able to:

- Explore learning opportunities
- Develop digital skills
- Build personalized learning plans
- Practice English and communication
- Explore technology and programming
- Explore career pathways
- Discover educational resources
- Find scholarships and training opportunities
- Set personal learning goals

# 5. Core MVP Features

The following features are the primary features planned for the first
functional version of Protisruti.

These features will receive the highest development priority because the
project has an October 2026 fellowship deadline.

---

## 5.1 AI Learning Companion

The AI Learning Companion will allow users to ask educational questions
and receive personalized explanations.

Users will be able to ask questions using natural language.

### Example

A student could ask:

> Explain photosynthesis in simple language.

Protisruti could provide an explanation appropriate for the student's
learning level.

A user could also ask follow-up questions to receive additional
clarification.

### Planned Capabilities

- Answer educational questions
- Explain difficult concepts
- Provide examples
- Simplify complex information
- Adjust explanations to the user's level
- Provide step-by-step explanations
- Encourage users to continue learning

### Goal

Provide users with accessible educational assistance whenever they need it.

---

# 6. Personalized Study Planner

The Personalized Study Planner will create customized learning plans
based on the user's goals and available time.

### User Inputs

The user may provide:

- Subject or skill
- Current knowledge level
- Learning goal
- Available study time
- Preferred learning schedule
- Target completion date

### Example

A user could enter:

```text
Subject: Python Programming
Current Level: Beginner
Goal: Learn Python fundamentals
Available Time: 1 hour per day
Duration: 30 days
