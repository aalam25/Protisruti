# Protisruti 

### A Promise of Learning, Opportunity, and Empowerment

Protisruti is an AI-powered educational and empowerment platform designed
to help women and children access personalized learning support, develop
useful skills, discover educational opportunities, and make progress toward
their educational and career goals.

The project is being developed as a fellowship project with a focus on
using artificial intelligence responsibly to address educational barriers
and create meaningful social impact.

---

## Vision

The vision of Protisruti is to make educational guidance and learning
support more accessible to women and children, particularly for people who
may have limited access to personalized tutoring, mentoring, educational
resources, or career guidance.

Protisruti aims to become an intelligent learning companion that helps
users:

- Learn
- Develop skills
- Discover opportunities
- Set educational goals
- Track their progress
- Build confidence

---

## Problem

Many women and children face barriers to education and personal development,
including:

- Limited access to personalized educational support
- Lack of affordable tutoring or mentoring
- Difficulty finding appropriate learning resources
- Limited information about scholarships and educational opportunities
- Lack of personalized career and educational guidance
- Limited access to digital-skills training
- Difficulty creating and maintaining structured learning plans

Protisruti aims to address some of these challenges through an accessible
AI-powered platform.

---

## Proposed Solution

Protisruti will provide an interactive platform where users can receive
personalized educational assistance based on their learning goals, interests,
skill levels, and progress.

The platform will initially focus on two groups:

### Children and Students

Students will be able to:

- Ask educational questions
- Receive simple explanations
- Generate practice quizzes
- Create personalized study plans
- Practice academic concepts
- Track learning progress

### Women

Women will be able to:

- Develop digital skills
- Learn programming
- Improve English and communication skills
- Explore educational pathways
- Explore career options
- Discover scholarships and free learning resources
- Create personalized learning plans
- Track their progress

---

## Core Features

The initial version of Protisruti will focus on the following features:

### 1. AI Learning Companion

Users can ask educational questions and receive personalized,
easy-to-understand explanations.

### 2. Personalized Study Planner

Users can provide their learning goals, current level, available study
time, and target period to receive a structured study plan.

### 3. AI Quiz Generator

Protisruti can generate practice questions based on a user's subject,
topic, and difficulty level.

### 4. Women's Skills Learning

Women can explore learning paths for:

- Computer skills
- Digital literacy
- English
- Communication
- Programming
- Professional skills

### 5. Career and Education Guidance

Users can explore possible educational and career pathways based on their
interests and goals.

### 6. Opportunity Finder

Users can discover educational resources, scholarships, free courses, and
training opportunities.

### 7. Progress Tracker

Users can monitor their learning activities, quiz results, goals, and
progress.

## Target Users

Protisruti will initially focus on:

### Students

Students who need additional educational support, practice, study
planning, or personalized explanations.

### Women

Women who want to continue learning, develop digital and professional
skills, explore careers, or discover educational opportunities.

---

## Responsible AI and Safety

Because Protisruti is designed to support women and children, responsible
AI and user safety are important parts of the project.

The platform will aim to:

- Protect user privacy
- Avoid collecting unnecessary personal information
- Provide age-appropriate experiences for children
- Clearly identify AI-generated information
- Encourage verification of important information
- Avoid making high-stakes decisions for users
- Provide safe and supportive educational interactions

Additional safeguards will be considered before any real-world deployment
involving children.

## Impact

Protisruti will measure success not only through technical development but
also through educational and social impact.

Potential impact measurements include:

- Number of women and students who test the platform
- Number of learning sessions
- Number of completed lessons
- Number of quizzes completed
- Learning progress
- User feedback
- User satisfaction
