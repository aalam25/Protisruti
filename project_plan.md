# Protisruti

## Project Vision

Protisruti is an AI-powered educational and empowerment platform
designed to help women and children access personalized learning
support, educational resources, skill development, and opportunities.

## Problem

Many women and children face barriers to quality educational guidance,
including limited access to resources, personalized support, technology,
and information about educational opportunities.

## Proposed Solution

Protisruti will provide an accessible digital platform where users can
receive personalized educational support, develop skills, and discover
relevant learning opportunities.

## Target Users

### Children
- Students who need additional learning support
- Students who want personalized study assistance

### Women
- Women seeking educational opportunities
- Women developing digital and professional skills
- Women seeking career and learning guidance

## Main Goals

1. Provide personalized educational support.
2. Help users develop useful skills.
3. Connect users with educational opportunities.
4. Make learning resources easier to access.
5. Measure the project's educational impact.
